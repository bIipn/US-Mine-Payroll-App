Billing questions

Who counts toward the price?

Nobody is counted. The price is per company, and every supervisor and everyone on the crew is included.

Do we need a card to start the trial?

Yes. Stripe takes the card when the trial starts, and nothing is charged for the 14 days. The first charge is $300 on day 15, unless the company cancels before then.

What happens on day 15?

The trial ends, and Stripe charges the card $300 automatically. After that it charges $300 each month, until the company cancels. The dashboard’s Billing page shows when the next charge is.

How do we cancel?

In the billing portal, any time. A supervisor opens it from Manage billing on the dashboard’s Billing page, or from the billing portal sign-in with the email the card was entered with.

Cancelling stops the next charge. Cancelled during the trial, nothing is charged at all.

What does a cancelled company keep?

Access to the dashboard to the end of the period already paid for, or to the end of the trial. The records on the crew’s phones stay on those phones.

While the subscription is open, a supervisor can export the company’s records from the dashboard: the PDF for an inspector, the Excel safety log, the payroll workbook and the payroll hours file. After it ends, the dashboard opens only its Billing page, and a supervisor’s phone can still make the inspector PDF and the safety log from the records on that phone. Export what you need to keep before you cancel.

A company can ask through the contact form to have its records deleted from the server. To start again, a supervisor starts the subscription again on the Billing page.

How does payment work?

Stripe takes the card on its own page and charges it each month. MineMuster never sees the card number: the server keeps only where the subscription stands, such as when the trial ends and when the next charge is. A supervisor changes the card in the billing portal.

Does the time clock cost extra?

No. The time clock and payroll hours are part of the plan. A company that runs the safety forms alone pays the same.

Can the crew use it during the trial?

Yes. During early access the apps are not in the App Store or Google Play, so we set up the crew’s app with you during the trial. If you want to look first, try the app in your browser.

Already a customer?

Change the card or cancel in Stripe’s billing portal, with the email the card was entered with. Everything else is on the dashboard.
