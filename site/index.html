  1. At the gate

    Clock in

    Punch in on the phone. It reads its location once, at the punch, to check you are on site.

  2. Before work starts

    Pre shift exam

    Examine the work area and sign the exam. A condition you find stays open until the date it was fixed is written down.

  3. Before you run it

    Equipment checklist

    Go down the operator equipment checklist for the machine. A Bad Order has to say what is wrong.

  4. When something is wrong

    Maintenance request

    Send the Bad Order to the shop. A supervisor assigns it, and it is closed out with remarks.

  5. Midday

    Lunch

    One tap starts a 30-minute lunch. It ends by itself, and the phone tells you.

  6. End of shift

    Clock out

    Punch out. The day is counted to the hundredth of an hour, the way a paper time sheet is figured.

  7. End of the pay period

    Hours for payroll

    The hours come out as straight time, overtime and double time, figured by the rules of the company’s state, before taxes.

Set up for each company

A company puts in its own name, logo, state and pay periods, and the app follows them. A website dashboard for each company is coming too.

Who it is for

Mine owners and superintendents, safety and HR people, and the crew members who work the shifts.
