Privacy

What MineMuster collects, why, where it is kept and who else handles it, for this website, the office dashboard, the crew app, billing and the contact form.

The short version

  • This website has no analytics, no trackers and no cookies.
  • MineMuster keeps a company’s records for that company, and uses them only to run MineMuster for it. We do not sell them or use them for advertising.
  • Location is read only at the moment of a punch, and once when a supervisor sets the boundary pin, never in the background. A punch’s location goes to the server with it, and the company’s supervisors can see it.
  • An hourly rate a person enters stays on their phone.
  • Stripe takes the card. MineMuster never sees the card number.

In this policy “MineMuster”, “we” and “us” mean MineMuster, which runs minemuster.com, the office dashboard at app.minemuster.com and the MineMuster app.

This website

minemuster.com sets no cookies and runs no analytics or trackers. It loads nothing from any other site: its fonts, images and scripts are served with its pages. Vercel serves the site, and like any web host it handles each request for a page, including the address it came from, in order to send the page back.

The demo at minemuster.com/demo is the app running with a made-up company. What you do in it is kept in your browser’s storage and sent nowhere.

The contact form

When you press Send, the form sends what you typed to MineMuster’s server: your name, your company, your email, your phone number if you gave one, and your message. We keep it there to read it and answer you, and use it for nothing else: it is not added to a mailing list. The server counts how many messages have come from an email address in the last day, and from one network address in the last hour, so the form cannot be flooded. For the hourly count it keeps only a scrambled form of the network address, never the address itself, and deletes it after an hour.

To have a message deleted, ask through the form.

The office dashboard

Signing in to app.minemuster.com takes an email and a password. Sign-in is handled by Supabase: the password goes to Supabase and nowhere else, and the emails to confirm an account or reset a password are sent through it.

The dashboard keeps the person signed in with the browser’s own storage, not cookies. The browser also keeps which company was open last, and, for a week at most, that the person came from this website to start a free trial. Nothing else is stored on the computer. Signing out signs out that browser.

Everything the dashboard shows is read from the company’s records on MineMuster’s server (see below). It shows no one’s hourly rate or pay.

The crew app

The app saves every record on the phone the moment it is signed. Once a phone has joined its company, it sends the company’s records to MineMuster’s server whenever it has signal:

  • punches and their corrections, with the reason for each correction, and punches that were turned down;
  • paid leave;
  • equipment checklists and equipment use;
  • pre shift exams and maintenance requests;
  • the roster (names, job titles, who is a supervisor and each person’s payroll ID), the fleet and the area sheets;
  • the company’s settings: its name, state, place, MSHA mine ID, logo, pay periods, the day the workweek starts, clock-in site, earning codes, whether it keeps time, and whether a whole section of an area sheet can be marked OK at once.

These never leave the phone: the hourly rate a person enters for their own pay estimate, the accounts and passwords on the phone, the phone’s own settings (such as its lunch reminders) and anything of the made-up sample crew. The lunch reminders and the notice that lunch is over are set on the phone itself; no notification service is involved.

Signing in to the company’s server takes an email and a password, as on the dashboard. The phone keeps that session so it stays signed in.

Location

Where a company keeps time, the phone’s location is read at the moment of a punch (clock in, start lunch, clock out) and kept with that punch; a clock-in goes through only inside the mine boundary. The point it was read at, how accurate the phone said it was, how far it was from the pin, whether it was inside the boundary and whether the phone said the location was simulated go to the server with the punch (and with a clock-in that was turned down), and the company’s supervisors can see them. It is read once more when a supervisor puts the mine boundary’s pin where they stand. It is never read in the background, and none is read when lunch ends. A company that runs the safety forms alone reads no location.

Billing

A company pays through Stripe. The card and the billing details are entered on Stripe’s own pages, and Stripe charges the card; MineMuster never sees the card number. Stripe tells MineMuster’s server where the company’s subscription stands, and the server keeps only that: Stripe’s ids for the subscription and the customer, its status, when the trial ends, when the period paid for ends and whether it is cancelled. Stripe handles what it is given under its own privacy policy.

Who else handles it

Three companies run parts of MineMuster for us, and see only what that part needs:

  • Supabase runs MineMuster’s server: the database and sign-in, in Amazon Web Services’ us-west-1 region in Northern California. The company’s records, the accounts and the contact form’s messages are kept there.
  • Vercel serves this website and the office dashboard.
  • Stripe takes the payments.

We give personal information to no one else, unless the law requires it.

A company’s records

The records a crew makes in MineMuster belong to their company. The company decides who is on its roster and what goes in, and each company reads only its own records. If you work for a company that uses MineMuster and have a question about your records, ask a supervisor there first; you can also write to us.

MineMuster is for businesses and is not meant for children.

Exporting and deleting

While the subscription is open, a supervisor can export the company’s records from the dashboard: the PDF for an inspector, the Excel safety log, the payroll workbook and the payroll hours file. After it ends, the dashboard opens only its Billing page, and a supervisor’s phone can still make the inspector PDF and the safety log from the records on that phone. Export what you need to keep before you cancel.

A company can ask through the contact form to have its records deleted from the server.

Records on a phone stay on it until the app is removed, or a supervisor clears the phone from the More screen.

Changes and questions

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions about it go through the contact form.
