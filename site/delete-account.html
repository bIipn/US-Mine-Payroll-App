Your account

Delete your MineMuster account

How to delete your account in the MineMuster app, on the office dashboard or through a supervisor, what is deleted, and what your company keeps.

Last updated November 23, 2026

In the app

Open MineMuster, go to More and tap Delete my account. The app shows what is deleted and what stays before anything is deleted.

If the phone sends to your company with your own company sign-in, deleting it needs signal and your passcode, typed again (or your password, if you sign in with email): anything the phone has not sent yet goes first, so your company has it. If the phone never joined a company, or is signed in to the server as someone else, your account is deleted from the phone after you confirm, with no signal needed.

Signed in with email, with no company roster to open yet? The sign-in screen has Delete this sign-in.

Clock out first. The app does not delete the account of someone on the clock.

On the office dashboard

Sign in at app.minemuster.com, open Your account and choose Delete my account. Forgot your password? Choose Forgot your password? on the sign-in page and we email you a link.

If you cannot sign in

Crew, with no email: a crew sign-in has no email, so there is nothing for us to write to. Ask a supervisor at your company to delete it: on the office dashboard’s Roster page they choose Delete sign-in on your row. It is deleted at once and the sign-in stops working. Your name stays on the roster and your records stay with the company, with the link to the deleted account cleared, and you can sign up again with the company code if you want to. A supervisor’s own account is deleted by that supervisor, as above.

With an email: ask us through the contact form. Give your account’s email and your company’s name. We write to that email to make sure the request is yours, delete the account within 30 days of your reply, and tell you when it is done.

No longer have that email? Ask a supervisor at your company to take you off the roster, and say so in the form; we check with the company.

Taken off your company’s roster? You can still sign in to the app, only to delete your account: the sign-in screen says so and offers Delete my account, and a crew member types their passcode again. This works even though you are no longer on the roster.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in: your email and password, or, for a crew member, your passcode and the address made up for the account.
  • Your requests to join a company.
  • On a phone, the app’s sign-in and the hourly rate you entered.

Your company sign-in stops working at once, on every phone and on the office dashboard. An account you made in the app on another phone stays on that phone: delete it there, under More, or delete the app.

What your company keeps

Your name on its roster, and the punches, paid leave, equipment checklists, pre shift exams and maintenance requests you made, with your name on them. The link from them to your deleted account is removed.

They are the company’s records. The company keeps pre shift exam records for at least a year, as 30 CFR 56.18002 has it, and the time and pay records the law has the company keep. This is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.

MineMuster keeps them for the company while it uses MineMuster, and deletes them when the company asks. A supervisor takes your name off the roster when you leave.

An account kept on one phone

If your phone never joined your company, your account is on that phone and nowhere else. MineMuster never has it. Delete it in the app, or delete the app, which removes everything the app keeps on the phone.

A company’s last supervisor

A company always keeps a supervisor, so the crew can still be let in and the records read. Its last supervisor makes someone else a supervisor first, on the dashboard’s Roster page, and then deletes their account.

When nobody else at the company can take over, the last supervisor closes the company from Your account on the office dashboard, with their password typed again. Someone taken off the roster in the last 30 days could still be put back, so closing waits until 30 days after the last of them was taken off. Closing deletes the company and its records, so download what the company has to keep first, on Exports. A company whose subscription is still running cancels it first, in Manage billing.

How long it takes

In the app and on the dashboard, and when a supervisor deletes a crew sign-in: at once. Through the contact form: within 30 days of your reply. Copies in the server’s backups are deleted when those backups expire.

Questions go through the contact form. How MineMuster handles information is in the privacy policy.
