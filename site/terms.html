Who these terms are between

These terms are between MineMuster (“we”) and the company that starts an account on the office dashboard (“the company”). The supervisor who starts the company accepts them for it. Everyone the company lets in, supervisors and crew, uses MineMuster on the company’s behalf. How we handle information is in the privacy policy.

The service, in early access

MineMuster is the crew app (the equipment checklist, pre shift exams, maintenance requests and an optional time clock), the office dashboard at app.minemuster.com, and the server that keeps the company’s records between them.

It is in early access. The iPhone and Android apps are not in the App Store or Google Play yet, so we set up the crew’s app with the company during its trial. Parts of MineMuster will change as it is finished, and we may add, change or take away features.

Accounts and the roster

  • Each person signs in with their own email and password, and keeps the password to themselves.
  • The company’s supervisors decide who is on its roster, who is a supervisor and who is let in with the join code.
  • The company is responsible for what its people do in MineMuster, and for the records they make.

Price, trial and billing

  • MineMuster is $300 a month per company, with the whole crew and everything in it included. Prices are in US dollars.
  • It starts with a 14-day free trial, started by a supervisor on the dashboard. The card is taken through Stripe when the trial starts, and nothing is charged during the trial.
  • When the trial ends, on day 15, Stripe charges the card $300 automatically, and then $300 each month, until the company cancels.
  • Payments are taken by Stripe, on Stripe’s own pages. MineMuster never sees the card number.
  • If a charge does not go through, Stripe tries the card again, and the dashboard stays open meanwhile. A subscription that Stripe ends for want of payment closes the dashboard’s supervisor pages until a supervisor starts it again on the Billing page.

Cancelling

The company can cancel any time in Stripe’s billing portal, from Manage billing on the dashboard’s Billing page or from the billing portal sign-in. Cancelling stops the next charge, and the company keeps access to the end of the period already paid. Cancelled during the trial, nothing is charged, and access ends when the trial does.

We may close a company’s account if it uses MineMuster against these terms.

The company’s records

The records the company’s people make belong to the company. We keep them for the company and use them only to run MineMuster for it.

While the subscription is open, a supervisor can export them from the dashboard: the PDF for an inspector, the Excel safety log, the payroll workbook and the payroll hours file. After it ends, the dashboard opens only its Billing page, and a supervisor’s phone can still make the inspector PDF and the safety log from the records on that phone. Export what you need to keep before you cancel. The company can ask through the contact form to have its records deleted from the server.

The company’s own duties

  • A mine keeps its own duties under the Mine Safety and Health Act and 30 CFR, including keeping its examination and inspection records and showing them when asked. MineMuster helps keep those records; it does not take those duties on, and it is no promise of how an inspection will go. Keep your own exports. During early access, keep the paper equipment cards and pre shift exams as well, until your crew’s records are reaching the dashboard.
  • The standards text in the app is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.
  • The employer keeps its own duties under wage-and-hour law. The app applies each state’s overtime and meal rules where it can and shows the rest as notes; a bargaining agreement can change them. Meal flags are for a supervisor to review, and nothing is paid or docked automatically. Check the hours before paying them.
  • MineMuster works out hours. It does not run payroll or pay anyone, and the pay estimate a person sees is an estimate of gross pay; the pay stub is the official figure.
  • The inspector PDF covers surface metal and nonmetal mines under Part 56. It does not cover underground mines under Part 57 or coal mines under Parts 75 and 77.

Using it fairly

Do not try to reach another company’s records, get around the sign-in or the roster, overload the server, or use MineMuster for anything unlawful. If you find a security problem, tell us through the contact form.

Limits

We work to keep MineMuster running and the records safe, but we do not promise it will always be available or free of mistakes.

Changes and questions

A change to these terms takes effect only after the company has been told of it, by email or on the dashboard, and the date at the top changes with it. The price of a running subscription is not changed without telling the company first. Questions about these terms go through the contact form.
